# RedCapeTech — Governance & Compliance To-do List

**Status key:** [ ] Not started · [x] Complete · [!] Blocked · [~] In progress

Update this file as work is completed. A task is complete only when the action has been verified and the supporting evidence has been saved in the approved company records archive.

---

## Phase 0 — Establish the company baseline

- [ ] Confirm the exact legal company name and registration number against CIPC records.
- [ ] Confirm company status and identify any outstanding CIPC filings or fees.
- [ ] Download and securely archive the incorporation certificate (CoR14.3).
- [ ] Obtain and archive the current Memorandum of Incorporation (MOI).
- [ ] Verify registered office and director details.
- [ ] Verify shareholder details and issued shareholding against company records.
- [ ] Confirm beneficial ownership information and applicable CIPC filing obligations.
- [ ] Confirm the company's financial year-end.
- [ ] Create a company compliance register with obligation, due date, owner, status and evidence reference.

## Phase 1 — Governance and minute book

- [ ] Establish a controlled company minute book.
- [ ] Create and verify the director register.
- [ ] Create and verify the securities/shareholder register.
- [ ] File incorporation documents and the MOI.
- [ ] Prepare an initial director resolution recording the company's administrative arrangements.
- [ ] Prepare a director resolution authorising the opening of a company bank account.
- [ ] Record material director decisions as dated written resolutions or minutes.
- [ ] Create templates for resolutions, minutes and decision records.
- [ ] Define who may approve payments, contracts and commitments.
- [ ] Establish a records-retention and secure-access procedure.

## Phase 2 — Banking and financial controls

- [ ] Open a dedicated company bank account.
- [ ] Confirm the account name matches the registered company.
- [ ] Route company income and expenditure through the company account.
- [ ] Define how shareholder funding will be classified (for example, loan or capital contribution) before recording it.
- [ ] Select and configure bookkeeping software (GnuCash or an accountant-approved alternative).
- [ ] Create a chart of accounts appropriate to the company.
- [ ] Set up separate income categories for each revenue stream.
- [ ] Set up expense, asset, liability and equity accounts.
- [ ] Create a monthly bank reconciliation procedure.
- [ ] Establish an invoice and receipt numbering/storage process.
- [ ] Define an expense-approval and reimbursement procedure.
- [ ] Set a monthly close date and checklist.

## Phase 3 — SARS and statutory registrations

- [ ] Access SARS eFiling and verify the company's income tax registration.
- [ ] Confirm the company's registered tax types and filing obligations.
- [ ] Determine whether provisional tax applies and diarise relevant periods.
- [ ] Assess whether VAT registration is required or appropriate under current rules.
- [ ] Assess whether the company must register as an employer before paying remuneration.
- [ ] Confirm PAYE, UIF and SDL applicability based on the actual employment and remuneration facts.
- [ ] Record tax registration references in a restricted-access compliance register.
- [ ] Identify the person responsible for each return and payment.
- [ ] Add verified SARS filing and payment deadlines to the compliance calendar.
- [ ] Confirm the current rules and thresholds with SARS or a registered tax practitioner.

## Phase 4 — Revenue and expense tracking

- [ ] Identify all actual and expected company revenue streams.
- [ ] Create a revenue register with source, date, gross amount, amount received, invoice/reference and bank transaction.
- [ ] Create an expense register with supplier, date, purpose, VAT treatment where relevant, amount and evidence.
- [ ] Record all company transactions in the ledger promptly.
- [ ] Reconcile receipts to invoices, statements and bank credits.
- [ ] Record shareholder loans and capital contributions separately from revenue.
- [ ] Record director/shareholder personal transactions accurately and avoid undocumented transfers.
- [ ] Review cash flow, unpaid liabilities and tax provisions monthly.

## Phase 5 — Director remuneration and payroll (only when applicable)

- [ ] Prepare a cash-flow forecast before setting remuneration.
- [ ] Determine the company's ability to sustain remuneration after operating costs and tax liabilities.
- [ ] Obtain tax/accounting advice on the appropriate remuneration structure.
- [ ] Prepare a written director resolution approving remuneration and its effective date.
- [ ] Complete employer registrations required for the actual arrangement.
- [ ] Configure payroll using the current SARS tax tables and applicable UIF/SDL rules.
- [ ] Generate and retain payslips and payroll calculations.
- [ ] Pay net remuneration from the company bank account.
- [ ] Submit and pay EMP201 obligations by the applicable deadlines.
- [ ] Complete EMP501 reconciliations when due.
- [ ] Issue IRP5/IT3(a) certificates where required.
- [ ] Reconcile payroll postings and SARS payments to the ledger.

## Phase 6 — Dividends and retained earnings (only when applicable)

- [ ] Prepare up-to-date management accounts.
- [ ] Confirm distributable profits and the Companies Act solvency and liquidity test.
- [ ] Obtain tax advice on dividends and any applicable exemptions or relief.
- [ ] Record the director decision/declaration and shareholder entitlement.
- [ ] Calculate dividends tax at the applicable rate.
- [ ] Submit the required dividends tax declarations and payment.
- [ ] Pay the dividend from the company bank account.
- [ ] Record the distribution in the ledger and retain supporting evidence.

## Phase 7 — Monthly close

- [ ] Reconcile all company bank accounts.
- [ ] Reconcile revenue and receivables.
- [ ] Review and classify expenses.
- [ ] Reconcile payroll and statutory deductions, if applicable.
- [ ] Review PAYE/UIF/SDL obligations and EMP201 submission/payment, if applicable.
- [ ] Review VAT obligations and submit returns, if registered.
- [ ] Review provisional tax provisions and upcoming due dates.
- [ ] Update profit-and-loss and balance-sheet reports.
- [ ] Review cash available after liabilities and tax provisions.
- [ ] Back up accounting data and supporting records.
- [ ] Record the month-end review and unresolved items.

## Phase 8 — Annual compliance

- [ ] Confirm the annual financial statement preparation and approval requirements.
- [ ] Prepare annual financial statements and supporting schedules.
- [ ] Complete the company's ITR14 by the applicable deadline.
- [ ] Submit IRP6 provisional tax returns and payments where applicable.
- [ ] Complete the individual ITR12 for the director/shareholder where applicable.
- [ ] Complete EMP501 and employee tax certificates where applicable.
- [ ] Submit the CIPC annual return and pay applicable fees.
- [ ] Review beneficial ownership information and update/file changes as required.
- [ ] Review director, shareholder and securities registers.
- [ ] Review the company's records-retention and backup process.
- [ ] Archive final signed statements, returns, assessments and proof of payment.

## Phase 9 — Optional growth and procurement readiness

- [ ] Assess eligibility and value of relevant MSME/SMME registration or support programmes.
- [ ] Assess B-BBEE affidavit/certificate requirements for the company's circumstances.
- [ ] Consider Central Supplier Database (CSD) registration if pursuing government procurement.
- [ ] Prepare a company profile and capability statement.
- [ ] Establish a company domain and business email.
- [ ] Maintain a controlled archive of contracts, invoices and supplier/customer records.

## Important controls

- Do not pay director remuneration or declare dividends solely because cash is present in the bank.
- Do not treat shareholder funding as sales revenue.
- Do not assume every company must register for every tax type; determine actual obligations.
- Do not rely on old tax tables, thresholds or deadlines. Verify the current year with SARS.
- Do not commit confidential personal, banking, payroll or tax records to Git.
